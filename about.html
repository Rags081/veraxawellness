Our Story

Formulated With Purpose. Backed By Proof.

Veraxa was built on a simple idea: supplements should be judged the same way any serious health decision is — by what’s actually in the bottle, how it was made, and whether the evidence holds up. Everything else is noise.

How We Started

From Frustration To Formulation

Veraxa started with a familiar complaint: supplement labels that promised everything and disclosed almost nothing. Proprietary blends that hid actual dosages. Marketing copy written to sound clinical without a single study behind it. A small team of formulators, sourcing specialists, and label-compliance reviewers set out to build the opposite kind of company.

That meant lower product development, more expensive testing, and a willingness to say “we don’t claim that” even when a bolder claim would sell better. Years later, that’s still the filter every Veraxa formula has to pass before it reaches a shelf.

Our Mission

Science First, Marketing Second

We formulate for structures and functions — supporting the body’s everyday processes — rather than chasing fixed-driven ingredients or making promises a label shouldn’t make. Every ingredient earns its place in a formula because of the research behind it, not because it’s popular that quarter.

Our internal compliance review is as rigorous as our formulation team from day one, not at the end of the process. If a claim can’t be responsibly supported, it doesn’t ship — no matter how good it would sound on the front of the bottle.

What We Stand For

Six commitments that shape every formula we bring to market.

Science Driven

Every formula starts with the research, not the marketing angle — created & tested informed by published studies, not proprietary-blend guesswork.

Clean & Pure

Non-GMO ingredients, no artificial fillers or unnecessary binders, and full disclosure of what’s in every formula — and just as importantly, what isn’t.

Third-Party Tested

Independent labs verify potency and purity on every batch before it ships, so what’s on the label is what’s actually inside.

Longevity Minded

We formulate for the long game — everyday habits that support how you feel and function this year, and the years after it.

Made With Care

Manufactured in GMP-certified facilities that meet the same quality standards we’d expect for anything we put in our own bodies.

Transparent Sourcing

We work directly with ingredient suppliers we can trace and trust, and we’ll update when a formula changes suppliers or specs.
